Epic discovery now carries what it learned into research and discussion, so each topic starts with the thinking behind it instead of a blank page.

## ✨ Added

- Per-topic discovery briefs — at the end of an epic's discovery, each topic gets a brief of soft decisions, rejected paths and open questions that its research or discussion reads in full when it starts.
- Reconcile advisory when a regenerated brief post-dates in-flight research or discussion — it shows a non-blocking notice and re-reads the new brief, never overwriting your work.
- Epic discovery session logs are now indexed into the knowledge base, so past discovery thinking can be searched by later phases and other epics.
- Re-entering an epic's discovery now briefs you on the recent sessions' thinking, with a one-line index of older sessions, so you resume the conversation rather than restart from the topic list.
- A light, ambient nudge when the conversation reaches convergence, inviting you to pull topics out whenever you're ready.

## 🔧 Changed

- Epic discovery is now a deeper, challenging design conversation that works through real decisions and records them plainly, and topics are harvested only when you ask.
- The discovery session log is now a running record that keeps rejected paths, soft decisions and in-session findings instead of a thin summary.
- Discovery session logs now live under `discovery/sessions/`, and a migration moves existing logs there automatically.
- Research and discussion entry for epic topics now start from the topic's brief, falling back to its description when no brief exists.
- The README's getting-started section now centres on `/workflow-start`, and the feature descriptions are shorter.

## 🗑️ Removed

- The hosted Workflow Explorer page and its link in the README.
