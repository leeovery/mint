Epic discovery now carries what you worked out into each topic's research and discussion, instead of handing over a bare topic list.

✨ Added
- Per-topic discovery briefs written when you harvest topics — soft decisions, rejected paths and open questions that research and discussion read in full as their starting context.
- A flag on research and discussion that warns when a regenerated brief post-dates work already started, shown as a non-blocking notice that never overwrites your work.
- Re-entering an epic's discovery now briefs you on where earlier sessions left off, reading recent sessions in full and older ones as one-line summaries.
- Epic discovery session logs are indexed into the knowledge base, so past exploration is searchable by later phases and sibling epics.
- An ambient hint that you can harvest topics whenever you're ready, shown once the conversation settles and never forced.
- A migration moves existing discovery session logs into `discovery/sessions/`.

🔧 Changed
- Epic discovery is now a deeper, challenging design conversation that works through decisions with you, rather than only mapping surfaces.
- Topic synthesis is now pulled by you when ready, rather than proposed by Claude as an endpoint.
- The session log's Exploration section is now a running record of the conversation, keeping rejected paths and answers to in-session questions.
- Discovery session logs now live under `discovery/sessions/`.

🗑️ Removed
- The hosted Workflow Explorer page and its link from the README.
