# Consolidation Tasks: Token Aware Diff Sizing (Phase 6)

## Task 1: One `r` regenerator for both verbs, with the keeper the only holder of its merge inputs
placement: phase 6
severity: duplication

**Problem**: Release and regenerate each write the review gate's `r` rule on their own: re-merge the merge inputs held for the notes at the gate, else regenerate from the size check through the run's sinks, keeping a chunked result's merge inputs for the next `r` (`perRunRegenerator`, internal/engine/release.go:1149-1163; `RegenerateFreshRegenerator`, internal/engine/regenerate_fresh.go:98-122). On the regenerate side the merge inputs also live in two holders: a closure-local `merged` the regenerator re-merges from, and the keeper's `merged` that `keepRegen` saves from on a failed `r` (internal/engine/chunknotes.go:65-70). Only 6-10's forwarding callback keeps them equal. The closure-local one starts from `RegenRun.Merged` (release.go:91-94), a snapshot pointer `chunkNotesKeeper.regenRun` copies from the keeper (chunknotes.go:83-85) when `RegenerateRun` and `gatePerVersion` build the gate's regenerator (regenerate_interactive.go:267, regenerate_batch.go:477). Beneath both, four generation sites chain the three notes sinks (`WithProgress`, `WithChunkNotes`, `WithMergeInputs`) by hand (release.go:1004 and :1156, regenerate_fresh.go:65 and :100-105); release passes the keeper's methods one at a time while regenerate uses `chunkNotesKeeper.sinks`. The verbs also re-merge through different generators, release through its unwired one (release.go:1159) and regenerate through its wired one (regenerate_fresh.go:113). That difference has no effect today, since `Remerge` (internal/notes/remerge.go:51-57) reports no progress and keeps nothing. A site that leaves a sink out, a change made to one verb's rule, or a write to one holder and not the other, compiles and passes every test. The operator sees it only when a failed `r` saves the wrong part notes or none, or an `r` on chunked notes re-runs the whole wave. It happened twice in this phase: release's `r` had no `WithChunkNotes` until 6-9, and regenerate's `r` ignored the chunk-notes sink and never updated the keeper until 6-10.
**Solution**: One `NotesSinks` method lists the three sinks once (e.g. `func (s NotesSinks) wire(g *notes.Generator) *notes.Generator`), and every generation site wires through it; release builds its sinks through `chunkNotes.sinks(...)` as regenerate already does. One `chunkNotesKeeper` helper owns the `r` rule: while `k.merged` is set it re-merges it, otherwise it runs the verb's size-check generation over the generator wired to `k.sinks(progress)`. `perRunRegenerator` keeps its `deps.Regenerator` and nil-generator guards and calls the helper with `GenerateWithContext` over this run's last tag; `RegenerateFreshRegenerator` keeps its first-release short-circuit and its `regenerating fresh notes for %s` wrap and calls it with `GenerateFromRangeWithContext` over the version's range. `RegenRun` stops carrying a snapshot pointer and carries a reader bound to the keeper, built only by `chunkNotesKeeper.regenRun`, so the keeper is the one holder; a reader rather than the keeper itself because the cmd-layer tests build `RegenRun` values, and a zero `RegenRun` must keep behaving as no held inputs. The closure-local `merged`, the forwarding callback and its nil guard go. Both verbs then re-merge through one generator. Derived from the finder's proposed shape (consolidation-findings-p6.md F1, F2); existing behaviour and test semantics are unchanged.
**Outcome**: A new sink, or a change to the `r` rule, reaches release and regenerate at once, and a failed `r` saves from the same merge inputs the `r` re-merges. Behaviour does not change: the existing tests stay green with their semantics untouched.

**Acceptance Criteria**:

- [ ] Rule: the existing tests pass with their semantics untouched. The only test edits are in the two cmd tests at cmd/mint/regenerate_remerge_test.go:73 and :83, which change how they build the held merge inputs. All five CLAUDE.md gates pass

## Task 2: One review-gate `r` assembly for both verbs
placement: phase 6
severity: duplication

**Problem**: Around an `r`, every review gate does four things: narrate an `r` that becomes chunked through the `regenStage`, hand on the lower-cap hint once the notes are shown, save the held part notes when the `r` fails, and after the abort warn of a failed save or name the saved file with the verb's own command. Release builds a `reviewRegen` inline and calls `reportKept` after the gate (internal/engine/release.go:588-595). Regenerate splits the same parts into three `RegenerateWriteRequest` fields (a pre-narrated `Regenerator`, `RegenShown`, `chunkNotes`; internal/engine/regenerate_write.go:129-147) that `RegenerateRun` (regenerate_interactive.go:260-271) and `gatePerVersion` (regenerate_batch.go:338-339, 465, 477-479) each set, and `reviewFresh` (regenerate_write.go:250-260) puts back together, reading a nil keeper as "keep nothing". The verbs also handle the `deps.Regenerator` test override in different orders. Release narrates it (release.go:589 over :1150-1152), while regenerate applies it after narration (`regenerateRegenerator`, regenerate_write.go:269-274). A site that leaves a part out still compiles and passes: a failed `r` then saves no part notes and names no file, or a halved `r` shows no lower-cap hint. Regenerate went without the save and the report from 6-8 until 6-10.
**Solution**: Two helpers that both verbs use. One builds the gate's `r` from its parts (e.g. `func (r *regenStage) review(regenerator Regenerator, k *chunkNotesKeeper) reviewRegen`, returning the narrated regenerator, `shown` and `keep: k.keepRegen`). The other runs `reviewGate` and then `k.reportKept(p, err, command)`. `RegenerateWriteRequest` carries the un-narrated regenerator with the `regenStage` and keeper in place of the narrated `Regenerator`, `RegenShown` and `chunkNotes` fields, so `RegenerateRun` and `gatePerVersion` pass only their stage and keeper; direct `RegenerateWrite` callers that pass neither get the gate as built. The helper narrates the `deps.Regenerator` override on both verbs, as release does today (release.go:594 with :1155-1157); regenerate applies the override after narration as built, and only that test seam sees the change. Derived from the finder's proposed shape (consolidation-findings-p6.md F3).
**Outcome**: The gate's `r` is assembled in one place, so a part added to it reaches both verbs, and no regenerate site can drop the save or the report. Behaviour does not change: the existing tests stay green with their semantics untouched.

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- [ ] A release `r` written after halving: once the regenerated notes are shown, and before the gate asks again, the lower-cap hint names the largest succeeded call of the run. An `r` that did not halve shows no hint (§10.3, §16.3)
- [ ] A single-version regenerate `r` written after halving shows the hint before the gate asks again. Under `regenerate --all`, such an `r` prints no hint of its own and counts toward the one batch-end hint (§10.3)
- [ ] An `r` that becomes a chunked run narrates as a chunked run on release, single-version regenerate and `--all` alike, and its notes line closes before the regenerated notes show. An `r` that makes one call narrates as built (§16.1)
- [ ] A failed `r` on chunked notes saves the part notes and names the file with the verb's own command: `mint release --notes-file <path>` on release, `mint release regenerate <version> --notes-file <path>` on regenerate. Under `--all` the batch then ends (§15.1, §11.4)
- [ ] A failed `r` whose part notes cannot be saved: the failure stays the AI's, and one warning carries the write error verbatim beneath it, on release and regenerate alike (§11.4)
- [ ] A direct `RegenerateWrite` on a fresh source that carries a regenerator but no `regenStage` or keeper: `r` consults that regenerator with the one-time context, a failed `r` saves nothing and names no file, and no hint follows the `r` (as built)
- [ ] With `deps.Regenerator` wired, `r` on release and on regenerate uses the override's body in place of the per-run regenerator (as built)
- [ ] Rule: `rg -n '\.narrating\(' internal -g '!*_test.go'` matches only inside the helper that builds the gate's `r`, and `rg -n '\.reportKept\(' internal -g '!*_test.go'` matches only inside the helper that runs the gate and the report
- [ ] Rule: the existing tests pass with their semantics untouched, and all five CLAUDE.md gates pass
